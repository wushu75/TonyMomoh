How I work

Research first, then build to think.

01 Get in the room

For CodeCatalyst I spent weeks embedded in a real private practice — recording calls, interviewing the practice manager and staff — to map how bookings, complaints and compliance actually work before designing anything.

02 Research that ships

I use field insight to define product strategy and MVP scope, not just slides. The point of the research is to change what gets built — and to be honest about what to cut.

03 Build to think

I'm a daily generative-AI user — LLMs and voice agents for prototypes, workflows and synthesis. I iterate between prototype and live demo fast, and build Chrome extensions in my spare time to stay sharp.

04 Ambiguity is the job

Early-stage, 0→1 environments are where I'm most useful. I'm comfortable defining a problem space from scratch and moving before everything is certain.
